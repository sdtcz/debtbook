Getting started

What is BashiBook?

A credit book on your phone, made for small shops and traders who sell on credit. Write down who owes you, remind them on WhatsApp, and see what has come back. It is free to start, and the core credit book is free.

How do I start?

Open bashibook.app in your phone's browser, type your shop name and start your book. You don't need to make an account. To keep it on your home screen, open your browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").

Which languages does BashiBook have?

English, Hausa and Yoruba. Change it in Settings.

Customers and credit

How do I add a customer?

On Home, add a customer. Type their name, and their phone number if you want to send them WhatsApp messages. Your customer does not need BashiBook.

How do I record a credit sale?

Open the customer, add a credit sale, type the amount and save. Their balance updates straight away. BashiBook then offers to send a receipt on WhatsApp, or you can skip it.

How do I record a payment?

Open the customer and add a payment with the amount they paid. Their balance goes down. BashiBook does not take or check payments for you, so record each payment yourself.

What is a credit limit?

The most you want a customer to owe. It is a warning, not a block: customers show a Near limit badge, and if a sale would take them over, BashiBook asks before recording it.

Can I track what I owe my suppliers?

Yes. I owe suppliers keeps your suppliers and what you owe each one, separate from your customers.

Can I give a customer a statement?

Yes. Open the customer and share a statement of their entries and balance, for example on WhatsApp.

Reminders and getting paid

How do I remind a customer?

Open the customer and tap Remind. WhatsApp opens with a polite message ready, and you press send. BashiBook never sends messages by itself.

Can I remind everyone who is late?

Yes. On Home, tap Chase all overdue. It lines up a WhatsApp reminder for each overdue customer, and you press send on each. Share on WhatsApp sends a short summary of who is late. For a daily nudge, turn on Chase reminders in Settings.

What is Pay me?

Add your bank details once, then send a customer a Pay me link on WhatsApp so they can see your account details and pay by bank transfer. You can also print a QR poster for your counter. When the money arrives, record the payment in BashiBook.

Offline, backup and safety

Does BashiBook work offline?

Yes, once opened. Open it with internet the first time. After that you can add customers, sales and payments even when the network is bad. Sending a WhatsApp message still needs WhatsApp to get through.

Will it use up my data?

Very little. The app saves itself on your phone after the first visit, and recording sales and payments on the free book uses no data.

Where is my book kept, and how do I back it up?

Your book is kept on your phone. Go to Settings → Export backup to save a free backup file. Keep a copy somewhere safe, away from the phone, and do it often.

I have a new phone. How do I move my book?

Export a backup file on your old phone and move the file to your new phone. Open BashiBook on the new phone and choose Settings → Restore backup. Restore replaces all the data on that phone, so BashiBook asks you to confirm first. With Pro, you can restore from encrypted cloud backup using your recovery code.

How do I stop other people opening my book?

Set a PIN lock in Settings. There is no way to reset a forgotten PIN yet, so keep your PIN safe and export a backup regularly.

Who can see my records?

Your book is saved on your phone. We don't receive your customers or amounts. If you turn on Pro cloud backup, your book is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded. Read more in Privacy.

BashiBook Pro

What does Pro add?

Encrypted cloud backup and restore on a new phone. The credit book itself stays free. You can try Pro free for 5 days, once per phone.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Go to Settings → Manage or cancel subscription, or open the Stripe billing page and sign in with the email on your receipt. There you can cancel, update your card and see your invoices. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Delete your data

BashiBook has no accounts. Your book is stored only on your phone. To delete it, go to Settings → Log out → Log out and erase this phone. You can also clear BashiBook's storage in your phone or browser settings, or uninstall the app.

Pro cloud backups are encrypted on your phone before upload. To delete your cloud backup or any purchase record we hold, email support@bashibook.app from the email address you used to buy Pro. We delete it within 30 days.

Anonymous usage counts contain no personal data, so there is nothing to delete.

Still stuck?

Email us at support@bashibook.app to tell us what broke or ask a question. In the app, Settings → Help & support opens the same email.
