Ready-to-send messages for shop owners. Tap Send on WhatsApp, or long-press a message to copy it.

Why a polite reminder works better

Kind words keep the customer and bring the money back.

  • Many customers mean to pay. A friendly nudge helps them remember without shame.
  • A polite message keeps the customer coming back to your shop, not to the next one.
  • Short and clear is best: their name, your shop, the amount, and thank you.
  • Send it in private on WhatsApp, never in a group or in front of other buyers.

Messages you can copy

Tap Send on WhatsApp, pick the customer, then change the words in [brackets] before you send. Nothing is sent until you tap send. Or long-press a message to copy it.

1. Friendly first nudge

Hello [Name], this is [Shop]. Just a friendly reminder that your balance is [amount]. Please pay when you can. Thank you!

2. After a week

Hello [Name], I hope you are well. This is [Shop] again about your balance of [amount]. Could you pay all or part of it this week? Thank you.

3. Before the holidays

Hello [Name], [Shop] here. The holidays are coming and we are getting new stock ready. Please help us by paying your balance of [amount] before then. Thank you, and happy holidays!

4. Thank you after paying

Hello [Name], thank you for paying [amount]. Your account with [Shop] is now settled. We appreciate you!

Questions

How do I remind a customer to pay without sounding rude?

Greet them by name, say who you are, state the amount plainly and ask them to pay when they can. Keep it short and end with thank you.

How often should I send a reminder?

Start with one friendly nudge. If nothing comes, send a second polite message about a week later. Always say thank you when they pay.
