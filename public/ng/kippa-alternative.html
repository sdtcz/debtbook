A credit book that stays with you

Your book stays on your phone, works offline once opened, free.

  • Records credit sales and payments for each customer, saved on your phone.
  • Works offline once opened, so you can keep recording in the market even with no signal.
  • Free to use: customers, balances, WhatsApp reminders, receipts and statement PDFs.
  • Free backup file you download and keep yourself, so you can restore your book.
  • Also tracks what you owe your suppliers, separate from what customers owe you.
  • App in English, Hausa and Yoruba.

Optional Pro ($1.99/month or $15/year) adds encrypted cloud backup and restore on a new phone.

Littafin bashi da yake tare da kai

Littafinka yana kan wayarka, yana aiki ba tare da intanet ba bayan an buɗe shi, kyauta ne.

  • Yana rubuta bashi da biya na kowane abokin ciniki, an ajiye a wayarka.
  • Bayan an buɗe shi, za ka iya ci gaba da rubutu a kasuwa ko babu siginar waya.

  • Fayil ɗin ajiya kyauta da kake saukewa ka ajiye, don ka maido littafinka.
  • Yana kuma rubuta abin da kake bin masu kawo kaya, daban da na abokan ciniki.
