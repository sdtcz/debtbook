Questions about paying

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No customer limit, no time limit and no card. The free book is not a trial.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

Your book stays on your phone and everything in Free keeps working. Cloud backup stops updating and CSV export switches off. Keep a backup file in Settings.

How do I pay, and how do I cancel?

By card through Stripe, billed in US dollars. To cancel, open Settings → BashiBook Pro → Manage or cancel subscription.

What if I lose my phone without Pro?

Restore from your free backup file on the new phone ("Have a backup? Restore it"). Without a backup file or Pro, a lost phone means a lost book.
