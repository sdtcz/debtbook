# Invite copy (marketing only)

The app's default "Invite a trader" message is plain English (`growth.inviteMessage` in
`src/i18n/locales/en.ts`), with Hausa and Yoruba versions in `ha.ts` / `yo.ts`.

The Pidgin lines below are **marketing copy only**: use them in hand-picked posts,
WhatsApp status updates or market flyers where Pidgin fits the audience. They are
not shown in the app. Always append a tagged link, e.g. `https://debtbook-neon.vercel.app/?app=1&src=<channel>`.

## Pidgin (pcm), Nigeria

> I don dey use BashiBook keep my bashi for phone. E free, e dey work offline once you don open am, e dey send WhatsApp reminder for you. Try am:

> Know another trader wey dey write bashi for book? Share BashiBook with am — e free.

## Plain English (app default, any country)

> I use BashiBook to keep my credit book on my phone. It is free, works offline once opened, and helps me send WhatsApp reminders. Try it:
